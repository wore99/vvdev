Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

KeepFlow Cut respects your privacy. The app is designed to edit videos locally on your iPhone or iPad.

KeepFlow Cut does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Photos and Videos

When you grant Photos access, KeepFlow Cut uses it only to let you choose source videos and save videos that you export. Your source videos remain unchanged unless you explicitly choose a system action that modifies your Photos library.

Video trimming, clip creation, previewing, ordering, and merging are performed on your device. Your videos are not uploaded to our servers.

Local Project Data

Saved clips, merged videos, and the project manifest are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. You can remove individual project items within the app. Removing the app also removes data stored in its private container, subject to your device and backup settings.

Permissions

KeepFlow Cut may request access to your Photos library to import, browse, and save videos. You can review or revoke this permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Analytics, Advertising, and Third Parties

KeepFlow Cut does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. We do not create user profiles or share your content with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version and its effective date will always appear on this page.
